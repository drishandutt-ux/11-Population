# Market & commuting statistics — compiled desk research

## London commuting (TfL / ONS, 2024–2025)

- 3.9 million journeys are made into central London on a typical weekday.
- Average commute time in London: 46 minutes each way (ONS Labour Force Survey).
- A Zones 1–3 monthly travelcard costs £197.60; Zones 1–2 is £171.70 (March 2025 fares).
- 27% of inner-London households have no access to a car.
- Cycling now accounts for roughly 4.5% of all London journeys, but 27% of
  City of London peak-time traffic is bicycles (City of London traffic count).

## E-bike market (Mintel / Bicycle Association, 2024)

- UK e-bike unit sales: ~160,000 in 2024, up 9% year on year, against a flat
  overall bike market.
- Average UK e-bike selling price: £2,150.
- 61% of UK adults who considered an e-bike and did not buy cited price as the
  main barrier; 34% cited theft risk; 22% cited nowhere secure to store it.

## Theft (Metropolitan Police / Bicycle Association)

- Roughly 18,000 bicycles were reported stolen in London in 2024; the true
  figure is estimated at 2–3x reported.
- Only 2% of reported bike thefts result in a charge.
- E-bikes are disproportionately targeted; insurers quote £180–£320/year to
  insure a £2,000 e-bike in inner London postcodes.

## Subscription behaviour (YouGov, 2024)

- 71% of UK adults hold at least one paid subscription; the median holder pays
  for four.
- 44% of 25–34 year-olds say they prefer "access over ownership" for expensive
  equipment they use regularly.
- Subscription fatigue is real: 38% say they have cancelled a subscription in
  the last 6 months specifically to cut monthly outgoings.

## Comparable pricing signals

- Swapfiets reports ~8,000 UK members; churn concentrated in months 2–3.
- Lime's average London rider spends £42/month; heavy commuter users spend £90+.
- When Buzzbike moved its e-bike tier from £99 to £75/month in 2023, sign-ups
  roughly doubled, but 90-day churn rose from 11% to 19%.
