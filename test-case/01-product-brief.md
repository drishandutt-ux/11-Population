# Loop — Internal Product Brief (v0.9, confidential)

## What Loop is

Loop is a monthly e-bike subscription for London commuters. For a flat **£65/month**
a member gets a Loop One e-bike (250W rear-hub motor, 70km range, belt drive,
integrated lights), **theft cover included**, unlimited servicing, and a
**24-hour swap guarantee** — if the bike is stolen, damaged, or faulty, a
replacement is delivered to the member's door within 24 hours. No deposit;
cancel with 30 days' notice.

A second tier, **Loop Cargo** at £95/month, offers a Tern-style longtail cargo
e-bike aimed at parents doing school runs in Hackney, Walthamstow, and Peckham.

## Why now

- TfL fares rose again in March 2025; a Zones 1–3 monthly travelcard is now £197.60.
- The ULEZ expansion has pushed thousands of older cars off London roads.
- E-bike theft is the #1 stated reason Londoners give for not buying an e-bike
  outright (a decent commuter e-bike costs £1,800–£2,800 up front).
- Cycle-to-Work schemes exclude renters-by-choice and gig workers.

## Competitive landscape

| Competitor | Model | Price | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swapfiets | Subscription (Power 1) | ~£75/mo | Dutch-style bike, heavy, waiting lists |
| Lime / Forest | Per-ride dockless hire | £3–6 per trip | Expensive for daily commuting, availability roulette |
| Buzzbike | Subscription (pedal + e-bike) | £29–80/mo | Theft excess of £99, slower swap |
| Ownership | Buy outright | £1,800–2,800 | Theft risk, maintenance, up-front cost |

Loop's positioning: **"Your bike, our problem."** The subscription absorbs the
three adoption blockers — up-front cost, theft anxiety, and maintenance hassle.

## Launch plan

Soft launch in **Hackney, Islington, and Southwark** in Q1, 500 bikes.
Partnership talks with two employers (a Shoreditch design agency and a
King's Cross biotech) for subsidised employee plans at £45/month.

## Open questions for the panel

1. Is £65/month under the psychological ceiling for commuters currently
   spending £150–200/month on TfL?
2. Does theft cover + 24h swap actually neutralise theft anxiety, or is the
   anxiety about the *experience* of theft, not the money?
3. Will car commuters switch at all, or is the realistic market only
   existing public-transport users and lapsed cyclists?
4. Is the Cargo tier a distraction at launch?
