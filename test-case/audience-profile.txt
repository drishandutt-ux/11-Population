London commuters aged 24-60 who travel into Zones 1-2 at least three days a
week. Mix of: current TfL users spending £150-200/month who resent the cost;
car and van commuters from outer boroughs who are sceptical of cycling;
lapsed cyclists who stopped after a theft or a near-miss; and a minority of
current cyclists who already own bikes. Include gig workers and shift workers
(couriers, nurses) alongside office workers. Income spread from barista to
consultant. A meaningful slice should be anxious about road safety rather
than price, and a hard core should be ideologically opposed to subscriptions
("ownership people"). Most are ordinary consumers, not analysts.
