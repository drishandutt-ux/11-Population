# Manchester food-waste caddy scheme — brief (TEST DATA)

This is a made-up briefing note written for testing the tool. Treat every figure as illustrative.

## What the scheme is
From spring 2026 Manchester City Council collects food waste from every household once a week,
separately from the black bin. Each household gets:

- a small grey kitchen caddy (5 litres) with a lockable lid, for the kitchen worktop,
- a larger brown kerbside caddy (23 litres) that goes out weekly,
- one starter roll of 25 compostable liners.

Households in flats without a bin store get a shared brown caddy in the communal bin area.

## What the council wants
- 70% of households putting the caddy out at least once a month within a year.
- Food waste in the black bin down by a third.
- Uptake to be as high in the poorest wards as in the richest.

## What the council can change (levers)
- Deliver liners free with the caddy and top them up on request.
- Send a text or leaflet the week before the first collection.
- Door-knock in blocks of flats with a caretaker.
- Change the collection day so food waste goes out with the black bin, not on its own day.
- Give a fridge magnet with what can and cannot go in.

## What the council cannot change quickly
- The size of kitchens in older terraces and flats.
- The number of shared bin stores in tower blocks.
- The collection contract (locked until 2028).
