# Market & employee-benefits statistics — compiled desk research

## Perks platforms (industry reports, 2024–2025)

- The UK employee benefits platform market is worth roughly £1.1bn and grows
  ~9% a year; Perkbox alone claims access for 4 million+ employees.
- Typical platform pricing: £3–15 per employee per month depending on bundle.
- Activation is the industry's dirty secret: across published case studies,
  **only 22–35% of enrolled employees open their perks app in a given month**,
  and the median employee redeems fewer than 3 offers a year.
- 62% of HR leaders say they struggle to demonstrate ROI on benefits spend to
  their CFO (CIPD reward survey).

## What employees actually want (YouGov / CIPD, 2024)

- 54% of UK employees say they would rather have the cash value of their
  perks added to salary; the figure falls to 31% when the perk's face value
  is at least 4x its per-head cost.
- The most-used perk categories are food & drink discounts (used by 41% of
  perk-app users), cinema/entertainment (33%), and gym (19%).
- 48% of 22–34 year-olds say a "genuinely good" perks package influenced
  their decision to accept a job offer.

## The surplus economy (WRAP / company reports, 2024)

- UK hospitality and food service waste roughly 1.1 million tonnes of food a
  year; 75% of it was avoidable.
- Too Good To Go's UK operation saved ~19 million meals in 2024; a typical
  surprise bag costs £4–6 for £12–15 of retail food and sells out at
  well-known bakeries within minutes of listing.
- 58% of UK adults say end-of-day discounts make them feel "smart, not
  cheap"; the feeling flips above the age of 55.

## Unsold event inventory (SOLT / industry, 2024)

- West End average seat occupancy was ~79% in 2024 — roughly 3.1 million
  seats went unsold across the year.
- Comedy clubs and mid-size music venues report 15–30% unsold capacity on
  weeknights; most have no channel to move it inside 6 hours.
- Rush and lottery ticket schemes (TodayTix Rush, National Theatre Friday
  Rush) sell out in minutes — demand for last-minute discounted seats
  outstrips organised supply.

## Location, privacy, and gamified rewards

- 57% of UK employees say they would be uncomfortable sharing live location
  with an app "connected to my employer", falling to 34% if location is
  processed on-device and never visible to the employer.
- The Gambling Commission has signalled interest in prize-draw mechanics in
  commercial apps; legal reviews advise "no consideration, no gambling" —
  rewards allocated free of charge with a skill-free draw remain promotions.
- 38% of UK adults have cancelled a subscription in the last 6 months
  specifically to cut monthly outgoings (subscription fatigue).
