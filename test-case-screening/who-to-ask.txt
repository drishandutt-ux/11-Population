Women aged 30 to 65 across England who are overdue for cervical screening and are being invited to order a free home HPV test kit. Many live in the most deprived areas, in London, and in northern and Midlands cities, and many are from Black, South Asian and other ethnic minority backgrounds. A mix of jobs, shifts, childcare, digital confidence and reasons for putting it off (embarrassment, pain, past trauma, time, privacy at home, language). Include a few practice nurses, GPs and community pharmacists who answer for the women they see. (Test description for a tool demonstration.)
