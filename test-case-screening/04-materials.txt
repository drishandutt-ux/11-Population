# Materials to test (TEST DATA)

*Invented wordings for a tool test. They are not the NHS's real messages.*

## Three invitations (for Test messages and the Reaction survey)

**Invitation A — NHS App message**
> You are due for cervical screening. You can now order a free kit to test yourself at home. Open the NHS App to order your kit.

**Invitation B — Text with a one-tap link**
> NHS: You can now do your cervical screening test at home, in private, in five minutes. Tap to order a free kit, posted in a plain envelope: nhs.uk/kit (test link)

**Invitation C — Offered in person at a GP or pharmacy visit (script)**
> "While you're here — you're due your cervical screening. We've got a kit you can do at home, in private, takes five minutes, and you post it back free. Would you like to take one now?"

## Three kit leaflet headlines (for Choose between)

- **A:** "Five minutes. Your bathroom. No appointment."
- **B:** "Most cervical cancers can be prevented. This test is how."
- **C:** "Nobody else needs to see. Nobody else needs to know."

## Two letters for a positive kit (for the A/B test)

**Version 1 — the current clinical letter**
> Your self-sample showed high-risk human papillomavirus (HPV). You need a further test called a cervical screening sample, taken by a clinician. Please contact your GP practice to book an appointment.

**Version 2 — the plain-language letter with a named nurse**
> Your kit found HPV, a very common virus that most people clear on their own. **This is not a cancer result.** The next step is a short check at your surgery to look after your cervix. A nurse called Sam will ring you within a week to book a time that suits you, with a female clinician if you would like one.
