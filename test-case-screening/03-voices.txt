# Voices — what women and professionals said (TEST DATA)

*Invented quotes for a tool test, written to sound like listening-session notes. No real person
is quoted.*

1. **Woman, 34, Bradford, two children, works shifts in a warehouse:** "The letter goes on the fridge and then it's been three years. If I could do it at home after the kids are down I'd do it tonight."
2. **Woman, 41, Newham, Bangladeshi, does not drive:** "My husband reads the post. I don't want a letter about this in the house at all."
3. **Woman, 29, Manchester, office job:** "I've had the NHS App for ages and never once looked at the messages tab. Text me a link and I'll press it."
4. **Woman, 47, Sunderland, survivor of sexual violence:** "A stranger with a speculum is the whole reason I've never been. Doing it myself changes everything for me."
5. **Woman, 58, Birmingham, Black Caribbean, retired care worker:** "I don't trust doing it myself. What if I get it wrong and they say I'm clear when I'm not?"
6. **Woman, 38, Leeds, larger body, self-employed:** "It's the lying on the bed. It's not the test, it's the being looked at."
7. **Woman, 63, Cornwall, rural, nearest surgery 40 minutes:** "I've done the bowel one at home, so why not. But if it's positive I've still got to get to the surgery, haven't I?"
8. **Woman, 32, Lambeth, gay:** "Every time I go they ask if I could be pregnant and look at me funny. I stopped going. A kit through the door would be fine."
9. **Woman, 44, Luton, Pakistani, three children:** "If the instructions are only in English my sister will have to read them to me, and then she knows."
10. **Woman, 52, Hull, learning disability, lives with her mother:** "I need someone to show me with a picture. Mum does my letters."
11. **Woman, 36, Croydon, nurse on nights:** "I tell everyone else to go. I'm four years late myself. Hand me a kit when I'm at the surgery for something else and it's done."
12. **Practice nurse, 49, Wolverhampton:** "Half my smears are booked, cancelled and rebooked. If they get a positive kit and then can't get a slot with a woman, we lose them at the last step."
13. **Community pharmacist, 55, Leicester:** "Women already pick up their pill and the morning-after pill from us. A kit over the counter would be the most natural thing in the world."
14. **GP, 46, Tower Hamlets:** "The worry for me is the positive result. 'HPV found' on a letter reads as 'you have cancer' to a lot of people, and then they don't come in at all."
