Women aged 30 to 65 in England who are overdue for cervical screening and are now being invited to order a free home HPV self-sampling kit, plus the professionals who meet them (practice nurses, GPs, community pharmacists, sexual health nurses), about one in six of the population, answering for the women they see. Spread across England, over-represented in the most deprived areas, in London and in the big northern and Midlands cities. A real mix of ethnicity (White British, Black Caribbean, Black African, Bangladeshi, Pakistani, Indian, Eastern European), of work (shifts, office, self-employed, at home with children, retired), of digital confidence (some never open the NHS App), and of reasons for not going (embarrassment, pain, past trauma, time and childcare, body image, not trusting a self-test, privacy at home, language, learning disability, feeling judged as an LGBTQ+ woman). Some have never been screened; some were screened once and stopped. (Test description for a tool demonstration.)
