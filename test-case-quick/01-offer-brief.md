# PedalPass — offer brief (TEST DATA, invented for a tool test)

**What it is.** PedalPass is a monthly e-bike subscription for London commuters. For
**£15 a month** you get unlimited 30-minute rides on docked e-bikes across Zones 1–3.
Rides over 30 minutes cost 10p a minute. There is no deposit and you can cancel any month.

**Who runs it.** A private operator in partnership with three London boroughs (Lambeth,
Hackney, Tower Hamlets). The boroughs provide the dock sites; the operator owns the bikes.

**What the operator can change.**
- The price (anything from £9 to £20 a month).
- A free first month.
- A bundled helmet, delivered to the door.
- More docks near stations (up to 40 new docks in year one).

**What the operator cannot change.**
- Road safety and the number of protected cycle lanes (that is TfL and the boroughs).
- The weather.
- Workplace showers and bike storage (that is each employer).
- Coverage outside Zones 1–3 in year one.

**The decision.** Launch at £15 in spring, or launch at £9 with a free first month and
raise the price later.
