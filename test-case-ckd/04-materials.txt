# Invitation and explanation materials (TEST DATA)

The messages and leaflet lines the programme is choosing between. Used in the Lab as the
material for Test messages, the A/B test and Choose between.

## Invitation wordings

**Letter (current):**
"Our records show you are due a routine kidney check. Please contact the surgery to book a blood
and urine test."

**Text:**
"Hi [name], it's [practice]. People with diabetes or high blood pressure should have a kidney
check once a year and yours is due. It's a quick blood and urine test. Book here: [link] or reply
YES and we'll call you."

**Phone call script (pharmacy technician):**
"Hello [name], I'm calling from [practice]. Because of your [diabetes / blood pressure] we like
to check your kidneys once a year, and it's been a while. It's a blood test and a small urine
sample. I can book you a slot at the surgery, or at [chemist] on [street] on the way home, or
there's a clinic in [place] on Saturday. Which is easiest?"

## Leaflet headlines

- A: "Diabetes and high blood pressure can damage your kidneys silently. A yearly check catches it early."
- B: "Your kidney check: two quick tests, once a year. Free, and it can be done at your chemist."
- C: "Found early, kidney disease can be slowed with one tablet a day. Found late, it means dialysis."

## Explaining a result (two versions)

**Version 1 (current letter):** "Your recent tests show reduced kidney function consistent with
chronic kidney disease stage 3a. Please book a routine appointment to discuss."

**Version 2 (proposed):** "Your kidney test shows your kidneys are working a bit less well than
they should, which is common with diabetes and usually causes no symptoms. It is not kidney
failure. There is a tablet that protects the kidneys and heart, and our pharmacist would like ten
minutes on the phone to explain it and answer your questions. We'll call you on [date]."
