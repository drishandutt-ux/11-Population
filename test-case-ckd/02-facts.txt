# Facts and figures — early kidney disease in Northmoor and Coast (TEST DATA, figures illustrative)

These numbers are invented for testing. They are shaped like real statistics so the tool can
read them as numbers, but nothing here should be quoted as fact. The live research pass will find
the real national figures; where they disagree, the real figure wins.
