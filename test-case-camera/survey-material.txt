## The concept, as it would be shown to a respondent

**Pip is a camera the size of a keyring fob that you can teach to do a job.**

Stick it anywhere with the clip, magnet or suction mount in the box. Open the Pip app, connect the
camera, and tell it in plain words what you want it to do. Examples:

- *"Watch my wardrobe and build me a private website, laid out like a shopping site, showing every
  item I own, what is clean, and outfits I could wear today."*
- *"Watch the fridge shelf and tell me what is about to go off, and what I could cook with it."*
- *"Watch the cot while I'm in the next room and tell me if the baby rolls over or the blanket
  covers their face."*
- *"Watch the front door and tell me when a parcel is left, or a person I don't know lingers."*
- *"Watch the dog while I'm out and tell me if he's on the sofa."*

The app picks a starting point from a library of expert-made and open-source vision templates,
then trains a small model for your camera on your own footage over the first day or two. It
learns your wardrobe, your fridge, your baby, not a generic one. You can correct it ("that's a
scarf, not a belt") and it gets better.
