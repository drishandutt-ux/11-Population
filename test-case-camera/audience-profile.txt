UK adults aged 24 to 70 who live in a home with broadband and own a smartphone, mixed across
England, Scotland and Wales, homeowners and renters, cities and towns. A mix of: parents of
under-threes weighing a smarter baby monitor; people who already own a Ring, Blink or Wyze camera
and are happy with it; tinkerers who have built something similar themselves; fashion-led 25 to 35
year olds who would use a wardrobe website daily; households that throw a lot of food away and
would set up the fridge job first; privacy-worried people who do not believe any on-device claim;
adult children looking after a parent living alone; renters who cannot drill; over-60s who want
someone else to set it up; and a hard core who think a camera indoors is never acceptable. Most
are ordinary consumers, not technologists. Income spread from student to GP.
