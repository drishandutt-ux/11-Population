# Early reactions from the March 2026 concept screening (TEST DATA — invented quotes)

Fourteen short comments from people shown the concept card and a two-minute demo video.
Names, places and details invented.

1. **Hannah, 34, Leeds, new parent, semi** — "The baby one sold it. The monitor we have just shows a picture; this would actually tell me if she's rolled. But it's going in the nursery, so I need to know for certain nothing is uploaded."
2. **Marcus, 41, Croydon, IT manager, terrace** — "Every brand says on-device. Then a year later there's a story. I'd want to see the network traffic myself before I believed it."
3. **Chloe, 27, Manchester, retail assistant, flat-share** — "A website of my own wardrobe like ASOS? Yes. I'd use that every morning. Not sure I'd pay monthly for it though, everything is monthly now."
4. **Derek, 63, Norwich, retired, bungalow** — "I've got a Ring on the door and that's plenty. Programming a camera sounds like a job, not a product."
5. **Amira, 38, Birmingham, GP, detached** — "The fridge one is the one that would save me money. We throw out so much. If it just said 'the spinach goes today' I'd cook it."
6. **Tom, 29, Bristol, software developer, flat** — "I've built this with a Pi and a webcam and it took a weekend. If this really trains in a day from a template, fifty quid is nothing."
7. **Priya, 45, Slough, teacher, semi** — "I'd get one for my mum's flat, to tell me if she hasn't got up by ten. That's not on your list but it's the one I'd actually pay for."
8. **Gavin, 52, Glasgow, taxi driver, terrace** — "Three-day battery. So I'm charging it twice a week. It'll end up in a drawer like the other one."
9. **Sophie, 31, Brighton, renting, flat** — "Can't drill, so the magnet and suction bit matters more than you think. If it falls off the fridge once, I'm done."
10. **Ken, 58, Sheffield, warehouse, terrace** — "What happens when it gets it wrong? False alerts at 3am and it's going in the bin, same as the last one."
11. **Leila, 36, Cardiff, marketing, semi** — "I like that it's one camera for lots of jobs. But 'ask it to do anything' makes me suspicious. Anything? Show me the list."
12. **Rob, 47, Nottingham, landlord** — "Could I put one in the hallway of a house I let? Probably not legally, and I wouldn't want the argument."
13. **Yusuf, 24, London, student, house-share** — "The dog-on-the-sofa one is funny but I'd use it. Forty-nine is a lot for a student though. Twenty-nine and I'm in."
14. **Margaret, 69, Exeter, retired nurse, semi** — "The idea that it learns my fridge is clever, but I'd want a person to set it up. I'm not connecting cameras on my own."
